Our Executive Team

Agency Leadership

Meet the dedicated professionals guiding the DSPPPPA towards a more secure, lawful, and transparent future for property development in Delta State.

Director General
Director General

Hon. Gibson Akporehe

Hon. Gibson Akporehe brings over 20 years of experience in public administration and property law. He leads the DSPPPPA with a vision of a secure, transparent, and legally compliant property sector in Delta State. Under his leadership, the agency has significantly reduced land-related disputes and illegal levies.

Director of Finance
Director of Finance

Ms. Efemena Igbrude

Ms. Igbrude brings her top notch experience in finance and accounting. She leads the agency's financial operations, ensuring that the agency's resources are used effectively and efficiently.

Director of Legal
Director of Legal

Barr. Reuben Onojewho

Barr. Onojewho oversees the agency's legal framework and enforcement strategies. With his extensive experience in property law and public policy, he guides the prosecution of property rights violators, ensures statutory compliance across all operations, and provides critical legal counsel to protect landowners in Delta State.

Director of Operations
Director of Operations

Mr. Kenneth Ashiponu

Mr. Ashiponu coordinates the on-ground task forces deployed across Warri, Asaba, and other key development hubs. His expertise in construction and site management ensures that field operations are handled professionally and safely.

SSA
SSA

Mr. Efe Akpokiniovo

Mr. Akpokiniovo manages the agency's communications, ensuring the public is educated about their property rights. He leads community town halls and develops campaigns that encourage citizens to report unlawful disruptions.

The Delta State Private Public Property Protection Agency is dedicated to safeguarding assets, ensuring public trust, and upholding property rights for all citizens.
