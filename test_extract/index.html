About The Agency

Ensuring Fairness in
Property Rights

DSPPPPA is a Delta State Government agency established to enforce the Delta State Public and Private Properties Protection Law, 2018.

We exist to ensure that every developer, landowner, and investor in Delta State can carry out lawful activities in peace — without fear of extortion, illegal occupation, or intimidation.

What We Do

Enforce Property Law

We enforce all relevant laws protecting public and private property across Delta State, removing illegal barriers and holding violators accountable.

Investigate Complaints

We provide a formal, accessible channel for individuals, businesses, and government bodies to report property violations and receive a lawful response.

Protect Development Sites

We work with security agencies to protect construction sites from illegal entry, occupation, vandalism, and extortion practices such as “deve” levies.

Support Investment

By creating a safe, predictable environment for development, we help attract investment, reduce project costs, and support Delta State’s economic growth.
