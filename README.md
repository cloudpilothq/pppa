## 🌐 About the Site

This is the official web presence of the **Delta State Public and Private Properties Protection Agency (DSPPPPA)**, a Delta State Government agency established to enforce the **Delta State Public and Private Properties Protection Law, 2018**.

The agency exists to ensure that every developer, landowner, and investor in Delta State can carry out lawful activities in peace — free from illegal levies ("deve"), harassment, unlawful interference, and extortion.

## ⚖️ What the Agency Does

- **Enforces Property Law** — Enforces the Delta State Public and Private Properties Protection Law, 2018, across all 25 LGAs
- **Investigates Complaints** — Provides a formal channel for reporting property violations
- **Protects Development Sites** — Works with security agencies to protect construction sites from illegal "deve" collectors
- **Supports Investment** — Creates a safe, predictable environment for property development and investment in Delta State

## 📜 Legal

The **Delta State Public and Private Properties Protection Law, 2018** prohibits:
- Collection of "deve" or any unauthorized development levies
- Harassment and molestation of property developers
- Demanding money before allowing work to commence on a site
- Forcing labourers to join community unions before they can work

**Penalties:** Minimum 2-year jail term and/or ₦1,000,000 fine for violators.
